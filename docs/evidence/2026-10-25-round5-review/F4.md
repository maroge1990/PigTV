Reviewed `230118c` newest first, against its parent and supplied baseline0255 `93583f6`. Mark's25 Oct acceptance is authorization for the proposed semantics; no further policy approval is required. The implementation remains **partial**, with three reproduced P2 gaps, one a new0259 regression.

## R5-F1 — P2: a previous hit still suppresses an ambiguous cancelled match

`recordingRules.js:309` unconditionally skips a candidate already present in `recording_rule_hits`, even when the new pairing logic deliberately refused the cancelled pairing as ambiguous.

Sequence: schedule and cancel same-title game at+2h; guide moves it alone to+6h (unambiguous, cancelled schedule stays at+2h and hit records+6h); guide then contains+6h and+10h. The cancelled schedule has two options and is correctly rejected by `sole`, but the+6h candidate's historical hit suppresses recording anyway. Only+10h records. Under the accepted “record rather than suppress” policy both possible programmes must record.

This pre-existing hit behavior is a missed0259 policy path, not a newly introduced mechanism. Repair must reconcile suppression with the current identity decision; do not simply remove deduplication and create repeated recordings.

## R5-F2 — P2: metadata-free pairing steals a still-present episode's schedule (new regression)

At `recordingRules.js:291`, `pairAirings` sees only candidates without episode metadata, but `pairInstances` loads *all* existing schedules. It cannot see that a schedule belongs to an episode still present in the omitted candidates.

Sequence: guide contains episode1.4 at+1h; evaluate, schedule it. Add a metadata-free same-title airing on the same channel at+2h, keeping episode1.4 at+1h. Pairing calls the existing episode schedule “vanished” and assigns it to+2h. The episode candidate is processed against its old+1h schedule before that move, so no replacement is created. End state: only+2h is scheduled; episode1.4 is missed. This probe passes on0258 and fails on0259.

Use the whole guide candidate set to establish presence/ownership, reserve episode schedules before pairing metadata-free candidates, and keep two actual airings scheduled. The contract's claim that episode-keyed airings are unaffected is not true in this mixed case.

## R5-F3 — P2: series pairing compares old positions instead of stable channel identity

`SCHEDULE_COLUMNS` omits `channel_stable_id`; `pairAirings.sameGroup` at263 compares `channel_item_id` with the current playlist position. Rule resolution and candidate identity do use the stable channel identity, so after a playlist reorder the rule finds the right channel but pairing cannot find its old schedule.

Sequence: schedule/cancel metadata-free airing on stable channel `ff`, position `pos_1`; reorder that same channel to `pos_2`; shift airing20minutes. Evaluation creates a new recording despite the same stable channel and an unambiguous move below3h. This also fails on0258, where20minute series moves were deliberately unsupported; it is an uncovered input of the newly accepted series policy, not a new raw-position mechanism.

Match channels by source+stable identity when present; never treat a stored position as an identity for a row carrying stable_id. Keep source/channel separation for unrelated programmes.

## Contract comparison

C-M now accurately states the intended accepted policy:15minute presence, one-to-one nearest moves,72h Sport horizon,3h metadata-free series moves on their channel, ambiguous cancelled pairings recorded, active prebuffer exclusion. It no longer promises universal durable cancellation. The three implementation gaps above mean it does not yet describe all actual outcomes.

The prose “one vanished schedule, one candidate” should clarify that ambiguity is checked over each candidate's eligible pairing edges (same title/channel, within movement limit), rather than globally across unrelated programmes. The code uses degree1 on both endpoints before consuming pending pairs. That is a conservative choice consistent with recording uncertain games; no defect is claimed merely because a global perfect matching could be inferred.

Sport candidates rely on `sportsEvents.eventsFor(hours:72)` for their horizon; old schedules are filtered to the same horizon. Series moves are measured from the stored schedule time; cancelled rows retain that time. These are heuristics, not provider identity. No physical/feed acceptance or cause of bugs5/8 is established.
